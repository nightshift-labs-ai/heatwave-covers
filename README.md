**HEATWAVE Cover Studio** turns your own photos into a multi-panel, sunset crime-epic poster and exports it as a PNG.
It is a static web app that runs offline in a desktop browser. Your photos are read on your own machine and are never uploaded.

Three steps:

1. **Add photos.** Pick one or many from your machine. Auto-fill puts them into the panels. With a single photo, every panel gets a different smart crop of it.
2. **Pick a layout.** Choose Key Art Nine, Hero and Six, Tilted Snapshots or Strip Five. Then pick a colour grade (Sunset Strip, Neon Night, Golden Hour or Miami Teal) and Poster Ink, which turns photos into illustrated poster art with flat posterized colour, ink lines and halftone shadows. Click a panel to select it, drag to move the crop and scroll or use the slider to zoom. Type the title, the script subtitle and the tagline.
3. **Export** a PNG at 1080 x 1080, 1080 x 1350 or 1920 x 1080. The preview and the export come from the same renderer, so what you see is what you get.

The design is our own: original lettering (Anton, Kaushan Script, Teko and Oswald, all under the SIL Open Font License), our own layouts and effects, and no logos, artwork, fonts or names from any game. The sample photos were made with an image model and show fictional people (see CREDITS.md).

Built by an AI agent (Claude Code) for Nightshift Labs.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# then open http://localhost:8080
```
